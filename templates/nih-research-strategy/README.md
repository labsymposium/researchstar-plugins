# NIH Research Strategy template

- 12-page limit for R01 Research Strategy (Significance, Innovation, Approach). Specific Aims is a separate 1-page attachment; References Cited is a separate attachment and does not count toward the limit.
- Format: Arial 11 pt (Helvetica, Palatino Linotype or Georgia also allowed), 0.5 in margins, US letter.
- Citations: Nature style, numbered and superscript. Typst uses the bundled `nature.csl`; LaTeX approximates it with `natbib` `[super,numbers]` and `unsrtnat`.
- The LaTeX version needs XeLaTeX or Tectonic for `fontspec` (Arial from the system).
